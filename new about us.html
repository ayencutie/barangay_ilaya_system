ABOUT US

Committed to providing quality healthcare for every resident of Barangay Ilaya.

Who We Are

Barangay Ilaya Health Center is a community-based primary healthcare facility dedicated to providing accessible, equitable, and quality health services to all residents of Barangay Ilaya. Through our Online Appointment System, we aim to make healthcare more convenient and efficient by allowing residents to book consultations, request services, and get updates online — anytime, anywhere.

Mission

To provide accessible, equitable, and quality primary health care services to all Las Piñeros by competent, compassionate, and efficient frontliners, ready to serve with fortitude and professionalism.

Vision

Ilaya Health Center is a center of excellence in primary health care which is systematic, self-sufficient, and technologically advanced.

Our Goals

  • To make healthcare more accessible through digital appointment scheduling.
  • To reduce long waiting lines and improve service efficiency.
  • To empower the residents of Barangay Ilaya to take an active role in their health and well-being.
  • Enhance coordination with community leaders and institutions.

Why Choose Our Online Appointment System?

  • User-friendly — book anytime, anywhere
  • Real-time schedule updates
  • Reduced waiting time for consultations
  • Safe and secure patient information handling
  • Strengthens community–healthcare connection
